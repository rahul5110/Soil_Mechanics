Laboratory Observation & Practical Inference

As water is poured into the 2 mm sieved soil, capillary tension draws water into microscopic pore throats, displacing trapped pore air upward as visible bubbles. Total pore volume is determined when soil reaches full 100% saturation without free water accumulating on the surface.
